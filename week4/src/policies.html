Safety policy

Every Ridgeline hike has a trained lead and a sweep at the back, so nobody is ever alone on the trail. Groups are capped at 15.

Before you book

  • Choose a trail whose distance and climb you can comfortably walk. If unsure, start with an Easy trail.
  • Tell us about any medical condition that could affect you on the hike.
  • Carry two litres of water, a snack, a rain layer and shoes with grip.

On the day

  • Stay behind the lead and ahead of the sweep.
  • If you need to stop or turn back, tell the sweep first.
  • We may change the route or the date if the forecast is dangerous. You keep your spot.

Privacy

We ask only for what we need to run a hike.

  • What we collect: your name, email address, the hike you choose and your group size.
  • Why: to hold your spot and send meeting details. We do not sell or share this information.
  • On this website: there are no advertising or tracking scripts. If you choose dark mode, your choice is saved in your own browser and never sent to us.
  • Your choices: email hello@example.com and we will delete your details.
