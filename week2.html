On the trail

Before your first hike

What should I carry?

Two litres of water, a snack, a rain layer, and shoes with grip. We share the rest of the checklist after you book.

Do I need to be fit?

Not for the Easy trails. If you can walk for two hours, you’ll be fine. Moderate trails assume you walk regularly.

What if it rains?

We hike in light rain. If the forecast is dangerous, we move the date and you keep your spot.

How much does it cost?

₹799 per person covers the guide, entry fees, and breakfast. Transport is shared between members.
